DRAW ORDER

HAND SLICE: pick the part (part list / ◀ ▶), press ▣ SLICE PAINT and drag over the pixels; ⌫ UNASSIGN removes them from any part. Brush 1 = single pixel. ↶ UNDO per stroke. Painted pixels are remembered: RE-SLICE asks whether to keep them. Saved with the draft / master.

Look at each angle, then AUTO-CHECK: parts with pixels, ≤2 islands, pivot at the joint and near/far on the right side are marked; anything flagged needs your eyes and a manual MARK.
